# Where the screenshots come from

The pictures in these screenshots are frames from *Tears of Steel*, (CC) Blender Foundation | mango.blender.org, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The film was downloaded from https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/ (`tears_of_steel_720p.mov`).

The transcript is the film's Croatian subtitles, `TOS-Croatian.srt` from https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/subtitles/, typed into the project with each line's words spread evenly over its time. Rezzo transcribes Serbian, Croatian and Bosnian, and the film is in English, so these screenshots don't show a real transcription.

The screenshots contain no sound, so the soundtrack's own license (CC BY-ND 3.0) doesn't apply here.
