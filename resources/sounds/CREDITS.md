# Sound effects

The sounds in Rezzo's library. Every one is under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): you can use them in your clips with no credit needed. We credit the people who made them anyway.

Each file was trimmed of silence at both ends, set to an even loudness (its loudest 400 ms at about −16 LUFS, peaks under −1 dBFS) and encoded as Opus at 48 kHz. The Freesound sounds were taken from the site's high-quality previews. `shared/sounds.ts` in the source lists them for the app.
